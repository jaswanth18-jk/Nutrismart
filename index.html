Turning Everyday Food into Better Nutrition.
Malnutrition has many causes, including food access, affordability, health and dietary diversity. Limited nutrition awareness can also make it harder to choose a varied diet. Many college and hostel students eat familiar foods without knowing the nutrients they provide.

Our Solution: NutriSmart helps you discover nutrients in familiar Indian foods, compare affordable alternatives, plan budget-friendly meals & improve awareness.

Explore Your Food →

🎯 Problem Statement

Some students may not know which everyday foods provide nutrients such as iron, vitamin A, zinc and folate. This awareness gap is not the only cause of malnutrition.

👀 Our Observation

Our project observation: some hostel students choose food mainly by taste and price, with limited attention to nutrient content. Support this observation with a student survey before making population-wide claims.

Students don't always need expensive food to make better choices. They need to understand nutrition in everyday food around them.
where nutrition awareness is limited.

🌱 Understand Hidden Hunger

Hidden hunger refers to inadequate intake of essential vitamins and minerals, even when someone gets enough calories.

Iron

Helps the body make haemoglobin. Sources include pulses, millets, leafy vegetables and meat.

Vitamin A

Supports normal vision and immune function. Sources include orange-coloured vegetables, some fruits and leafy greens.

Zinc

Supports growth, immunity and wound healing. Sources include pulses, nuts, seeds and animal foods.

Folate

Helps the body make DNA and red blood cells. Sources include green leafy vegetables and pulses.

Food sources contribute differently and absorption varies. This is educational information, not a diagnosis or treatment plan.

💡 Food Substitution - Affordable Alternatives

Why Substitute?

Alternatives are not nutritionally identical, but they give similar key nutrients at much lower cost and are locally available. Always check protein, iron & calcium differences.

Examples:
Almonds → Groundnuts
Chicken → Soybeans / Green Gram
Apple → Banana / Guava
Expensive Protein → Pulses
